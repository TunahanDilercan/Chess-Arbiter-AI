No account or login is required; the app works fully offline.

How to review:
1. Launch the app and accept the first-run notice.
2. On the home screen tap "Örneği aç" (Open sample). The sample game contains an illegal move: move 25 for Black ("gxh6") is marked red.
3. Tap "Kural dışı hamle" (Illegal move) and then "Ne yapmalıyım?" for the FIDE 7.5 penalty steps. Tap "Talep kontrolü" (Claim check) for the threefold-repetition claim.
4. Pro subscription screen: Settings (gear icon) > "Hakem Masası Pro". Each Pro tool can be used 3 times per week for free; after that the verdict headline stays visible and details are locked. Purchases can be tested with a Sandbox account. "Satın alımları geri yükle" restores purchases; "Aboneliği yönet" opens subscription management.
5. Settings > "Tüm verileri sil" deletes all data stored on the device.

The optional photo (OCR) input is disabled in this build, so the camera and photo library are never requested.
The app's interface is Turkish; FIDE article numbers are shown with every verdict. The app is not affiliated with FIDE.
