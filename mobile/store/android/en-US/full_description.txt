Hakem Masası is the arbiter's analysis desk for over-the-board chess. Play the game on the board, type it from the scoresheet with a dedicated chess keyboard, or paste PGN: every move is checked against the FIDE Laws of Chess and each verdict cites the relevant article. Works fully offline, no account needed.

The app's interface is in Turkish. Scoresheets in English, Turkish, German, French and Spanish notation are supported.

DURING A DISPUTE
• Illegal move: explains why a move is illegal and shows the 7.5 penalty steps for standard, rapid and blitz.
• Draw claims: verifies threefold repetition and 50-move claims, including the intended move, and separates claimable draws from automatic ones (fivefold repetition, 75 moves).
• Flag fall: checks whether the opponent has mating material.
• Scoresheet comparison: finds the first move where two scoresheets disagree.
• PDF arbiter report with articles and timestamps.

RELIABLE
• Move validation tested against independent reference programs on more than 16,000 real tournament games and 118 million positions.
• Never guesses between two legal moves; the arbiter decides.
• Your games stay on your device. No ads, no analytics.

FREE AND PRO
Move entry, legality checking, the rules timeline and PGN sharing are always free. Pro tools can be tried 3 times a week for free.
Hakem Masası Pro is an auto-renewable subscription: Tournament Week (weekly), Monthly or Yearly (7-day free trial for eligible users). Payment is charged to your Google Play account. The subscription renews automatically until cancelled; manage or cancel it any time in Google Play Store > Payments & subscriptions. After cancelling, Pro stays active until the end of the paid period.
