# Piece sets

All files are taken from the lichess-org/lila distribution (`public/piece/<set>`)
and flattened with `tool/normalize_pieces.py` (CSS classes → attributes, inherited
gradient stops inlined). The artwork itself is unchanged.

| `spatial/` | Spatial | Maurizio Monge | MIT — `LICENSE-MIT-chess-art.txt` |
| `fantasy/` | Fantasy | Maurizio Monge | MIT — `LICENSE-MIT-chess-art.txt` |
| `chessnut/` | Chessnut | Alexis Luengas (https://github.com/LexLuengas/chessnut-pieces) | Apache-2.0 — `LICENSE-Apache-2.0.txt` |

The chess-art README states the sets may be used "in your free or paid site
program or app, as long as the author is cited". Both licenses are shown in the
app under Settings → Licenses.

Do NOT add GPL/AGPL (cburnett, merida, mono, mpchess…), share-alike or
non-commercial (CC BY-NC-SA: staunty, california, maestro, horsey…) sets: the app
is a paid App Store product. See lila COPYING.md for each set's license.
